Tareeqa

  1. Statement mein bank statement ki PDF daalein. Password wali PDF bhi khulti hai.
  2. Screenshots mein customers ke payment screenshots daalein, ya Ctrl+V se paste karein. Amount, date, naam, bank aur TID khud parh liye jaate hain; ghalat ho to wahin theek kar dein.
  3. Har screenshot usi tareekh aur usi amount wali statement line se jor diya jaata hai, aur statement par us line par nishaan lag jaata hai.
  4. Hisaab mein apne register ya parchi ki raqamein likhein. Har raqam check hoti hai ke us ka screenshot bhi hai aur woh bank mein bhi aayi.
  5. Report se tasdeeq report print, PDF ya WhatsApp text mein lein.
